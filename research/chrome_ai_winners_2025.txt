When we launched the Google Chrome Built-in AI Challenge 2025,
our second annual built-in AI challenge, we invited you to develop web
applications and Chrome Extensions that used one or more built-in AI APIs.
Developers were invited to build
multimodal applications with the
Prompt API and implement hybrid strategies so
users everywhere could benefit from your applications.
Thank you to everyone who signed up and entered: over 14,000 people registered,
with more than 1,300 submissions!
Our judges were so excited to look at the functionality, your videos, and
code. We narrowed down our winners based on five criteria, including
functionality, purpose, content, user experience, and technical execution.
Congratulations to our winners.
Most helpful application
The most helpful winners effectively address a significant, real-world user need or problem. They combine practicality, user-friendliness, and technological excellence to deliver substantial and meaningful value to its users, making their daily browsing tasks easier, faster, or more accessible.
Chrome Extension: Nutshell: Hands-Free Web Access for Everyone helps users with motor disabilities to browse the web hands-free.
Web application: AAC Board AI helps people communicate, using Chrome's Built-in AI APIs to proofread, rephrase, and translate messages instantly, privately and offline.
Best multimodal AI application
The best multimodal AI winners use the Prompt API with multimodal capabilities, such as audio and image inputs. They effectively address a significant need or problem by combining practicality, usability, and technological excellence to deliver meaningful value to its users.
Chrome Extension: Phonaify detects English pronunciation errors and provides real-time phonetic feedback as users read online.
Web application: Pet Shelter App helps under-resourced shelters find forever homes for more pets.
Best hybrid AI application
The best hybrid AI winners seamlessly integrate client-side and server-side AI capabilities using the Firebase AI Logic or Gemini Developer API. There is a clear reason why the submission benefits from a hybrid AI approach, and they help address a significant need or problem.
Chrome Extension: Mentelo-An Interactive Chrome Extension You Can Talk To
offers instant on-page tech support to answer your questions, along with 10
other essential everyday features.
Web application: Trail
acts as your offline trail companion. Plan, navigate, and explore with
confidence, even without the internet.
Honorable mentions
These winners demonstrate significant merit and had noteworthy qualities
worthy of distinction.
BrowseBack gives your browser a photographic memory.
EduAdapt turns any web page into a neurodivergent-friendly reading experience, tailored for ADHD and dyslexia students.
Helix AI is a sidebar chatbot that lets you prompt, talk to your site, and attach images.
Marionette - The On-Device Multimodal Al Agent automates browser interactions, using the Prompt API, Summarizer API, Rewriter API, and Writer API.
The Turing Werewolf turns the classic social deduction game, Werewolf, into a solo experience using the Prompt API, where each player you meet debates and can deceive you.
In addition, thank you to everyone who gave feedback on their experience using the built-in AI APIs. Special thanks to Jay P., Jackson K., Akinwumi M., Anushka R., and Kyu L. for their excellent and detailed feedback to help improve our implementation.
